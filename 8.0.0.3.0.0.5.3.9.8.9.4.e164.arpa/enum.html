★ HOW TO REGISTER AN ENUM DOMAIN IN GERMANY ★
A SMALL GUIDE TO GERMAN ENUM REGISTRATION
------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT IS ENUM?
ENUM BASICALLY STANDS FOR TELEPHONE NUMBER MAPPING. IT LINKS TELEPHONE NUMBERS TO INTERNET-BASED SERVICES USING THE DNS.

IN GERMANY, ENUM DOMAINS ARE REGISTERED UNDER .9.4.e164.arpa. YOU GET AN ENUM DOMAIN BY REVERSING THE DIGITS OF YOUR TELEPHONE NUMBER AND SEPARATING THEM WITH DOTS.

SEE DENIC.DE FOR MORE INFO
WHY SHOULD I GET AN ENUM DOMAIN?
ENUM IS DYING. LET'S CHANGE THAT.

ENUM DOMAINS ARE A RARE PIECE OF INTERNET HISTORY THAT IS SLOWLY DISAPPEARING. ACCORDING TO THE OFFICIAL DENIC ENUM ANNUAL REPORT 2025 , ONLY 71 ENUM DOMAINS REMAINED REGISTERED IN GERMANY AT THE END OF 2025, DOWN FROM 80 AT THE END OF 2024. DENIC EXPECTS THIS DECLINE TO CONTINUE IN THE COMING YEARS.

HAVE YOU EVER WANTED TO BE PART OF A STATISTIC? HERE IS YOUR CHANCE!

GET YOUR OWN ENUM DOMAIN AND BECOME PART OF THIS TINY, ENDANGERED CORNER OF THE INTERNET. LET'S CONFUSE THE STATISTICS A LITTLE AND GET MORE ENUM DOMAINS REGISTERED!
01 / CHECK YOUR PHONE NUMBER
YOU MUST BE THE LEGITIMATE HOLDER OF THE TELEPHONE NUMBER YOU WANT TO REGISTER.

YOU CAN REGISTER ELIGIBLE GEOGRAPHIC NUMBERS, MOBILE NUMBERS, AND CERTAIN OTHER NATIONAL NUMBER RANGES LIKE SERVICE NUMBERS (0180), PERSONAL NUMBERS (0700), FREE NUMBERS (0800) ETC.

YOUR NUMBER MUST ALSO BE ELIGIBLE UNDER THE CURRENT DENIC ENUM GUIDELINES .
02 / PREPARE YOUR DOCUMENTS
BEFORE REGISTERING YOUR ENUM DOMAIN, YOU NEED THE FOLLOWING INFORMATION:

01. YOUR TELEPHONE NUMBER (DUH)
02. PROOF THAT YOU ARE ENTITLED TO USE THE NUMBER (COULD BE AN INVOICE, ORDER CONFIRMATION ETC.)
03. THE HOSTNAMES OF YOUR DESIRED NAMESERVERS
03 / CONTACT SPEEDPARTNER.DE
