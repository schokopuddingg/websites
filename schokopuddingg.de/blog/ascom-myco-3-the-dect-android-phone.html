Overview

I bought this phone after I saw a few people running aroud chaos events with it. It's an Android 10 phone lackig a cellular modem, instead having a DECT Chipset for telephony and communication within a DECT network. I already tested the device within the Eventphone DECT Network at many CCC Events. Connection is possible, the DECT reception is kind of poor tho.

In terms of technical specifications, the Myco 3 is powered by a Mediatek MT6763 chipset, has a hot-swappable 2935 mAh battery pack and a 5" Full HD screen. It is also equipped with a 1D and 2D code scanner and three programmable buttons. It is also IP67 dust and water resistant. You can find a detailed specsheet here.

Configuration

Overview

To configure and set up the special featuers, you have the "Ascom Settings" menu within the Android Settings app.

Wi-Fi

The Wi-Fi menu is locked behind a password, which is set to "40020" by default. Huge thanks to Joffi who took apart the apk to find out the password!

Ascom VoIP

Here you can configure the Myco 3 as a sip phone. In the newest version (Ascom Experience 3.6.6) you can even set up multiple sip accounts at once.
