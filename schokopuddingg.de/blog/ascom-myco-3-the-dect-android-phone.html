Ascom Myco 3 - The DECT Android Phone

The Ascom Myco 3 is one of those devices that feels like a niche product from the edge of telecom history: a DECT handset, but with an Android-based user experience and the kind of design language that screams business hardware.

It is not a consumer phone in the usual sense. Instead, it fits into the world of enterprise and professional communication, where call handling, reliability, and a very specific workflow matter more than flashy features or app ecosystems.

The interesting part is the blend of very traditional DECT telephony with more modern software expectations. In practice, the device feels like a hardened communication terminal: practical, compact, and designed to do a very specific job without much fuss.

For anyone interested in telecom hardware, it is a fascinating example of how DECT devices evolved beyond the simple cordless handset and into the era of embedded smart interfaces.
