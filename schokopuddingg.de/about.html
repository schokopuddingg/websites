Who am I?

My name is Schokopuddingg, and I've been a tech geek for as long as I can remember. Even as a kid, I was fascinated by old devices — cameras, tape recorders, broadcast gear, and anything that blinks, beeps, or buzzes.

Today, I'm deeply into broadcast and video technology, radio, smart home systems, and both analog and digital photography. You can find some of my stuff on my pixelfed instance.

Just don't leave my unsupervised for too long or I will randomly start building tv studios.

You can find me on various chaos related events and in hackspaces mostly around Munich and Nuremberg.

I collect CDs, vinyl records, retro tech gadgets, and old cameras. I listen to a lot of experimental stuff, such as Death Industrial, Dark Ambient, but also Rock, Metal, Pop Punk, 80s and earlier. This is my bandcamp collection .
